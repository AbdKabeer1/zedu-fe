# Zedu Product Context

## Product

Zedu is a cross-platform learning and collaboration workspace for schools, bootcamps, universities, training programs, and cohort-based communities. It brings structured channels, direct messages, live voice and video, files, search, notifications, and organization administration into one environment.

## Purpose

Help educators, administrators, and learners communicate, collaborate, and learn without losing context across disconnected tools. Zedu prioritizes structure over noise, quick onboarding, real-time collaboration, and role-aware governance.

## Users

- Educators and instructors running classes, cohorts, and discussions.
- Program administrators coordinating organizations, members, permissions, and communication.
- Learners who need clear spaces for updates, questions, resources, and peer collaboration.
- Institution operations leads evaluating scale, governance, support, and cross-platform continuity.

## Core value

Fewer tools, smarter learning: replace scattered communication and resources with one structured workspace organized around cohorts, subjects, classes, or projects.

## Current capabilities

- Channels, direct messages, threads, reactions, mentions, and rich text.
- Buzz voice and video meetings, screen sharing, chat, and recordings.
- File organization, sharing, preview, filtering, and search.
- Organization onboarding, invitations, member roles, billing, and settings.
- Web, iOS, Android, and desktop clients with real-time synchronization.

## Platform

Responsive web application and public marketing site, complemented by native mobile and desktop clients.

## Brand commitments

- Education-first, clear, capable, and welcoming rather than corporate or overly technical.
- Honest product language; do not invent customer proof, performance metrics, partnerships, or unsupported automation.
- Public marketing uses a light violet-and-white visual system, rounded surfaces, concise copy, and direct calls to action.

## Business actions

Public marketing should help qualified visitors either contact sales for a guided conversation or create an account to start for free.
